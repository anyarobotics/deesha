What we build

Five categories of special-purpose machines.

Each system is engineered around the client's process — from a single test rig to a full production line fixture.

01

Air delivery measurement systems

Automatic 1-axis and 4-axis test rigs for ceiling and table fans, with HMI control and repeatable airflow measurement.

02

Fogging & canon sprayer systems

Dust-suppression and fogging equipment engineered for underground and open-cast mining operations.

03

Cut-to-length machines

Precision coil-to-length cutting lines for transformer core and winding production.

04

Two-drum & salvage winders

Web-handling and rewinding systems for paper mill finishing lines.

05

Pneumatic & hydraulic refurbishment

Overhaul and recommissioning of pneumatic and hydraulic systems to keep plant lines running.
